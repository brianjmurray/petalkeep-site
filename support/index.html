Support

How can we help?

For PetalKeep support, email support@petalkeep.app. Include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

Does PetalKeep require an account?

No. PetalKeep works without login, accounts, or cloud sync. Your habit garden is stored on your device.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting the app can remove local PetalKeep data from your device. Make sure your device is backed up if you want the best chance of restoring app data later.

Can I move my data to another device?

PetalKeep stores data locally. Device-to-device transfer depends on Apple's backup and restore tools.

Why did my plant wilt?

Plants reflect habit consistency. Missed check-ins can cause wilt, droop, or scorch states, and returning to the habit lets the plant recover.

Does PetalKeep have subscriptions or ads?

No. PetalKeep is a one-time purchase with no subscriptions, ads, or in-app purchases.
