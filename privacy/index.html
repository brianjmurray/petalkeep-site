Privacy Policy

PetalKeep — Last updated: May 23, 2026

PetalKeep does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information.

All habit data, plant states, and streaks are stored exclusively on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. No habit data is sent to any server. No analytics are collected. No third-party SDKs that collect data are used.

PetalKeep does not use advertising networks, analytics platforms, or third-party crash reporting tools.

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer receives only anonymized aggregate sales data from App Store Connect.

If you have questions, contact support@petalkeep.app.
